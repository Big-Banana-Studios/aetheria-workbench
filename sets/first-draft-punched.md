# Beige

## Bit 1 — the room
I had a look at the room on the way in. {pace} You are all very well put together. The same three photos, the same soft light, the same little life: carefully selected, ethically sourced, mindful. {aside} The dust back here smells like old tobacco and forgotten ambition. Everybody pretending they have it together. Everybody. Me too; I ironed the patch. It is not a life. {deadpan} It is a hamster wheel made of veneer.

## Bit 2 — the party hat
Somebody took a bull. A big, terrifying, beautiful animal. And they put a party hat on it. (beat) Then they fed it organic kale and called it a wellness journey. That is us. We took the part of being alive that bleeds and fights and wants, and we made it cute. Manageable. Scented. The thunder went out and the wind chimes came in. {aside} That bulb has been buzzing all night like it has something to add. And we call ourselves resilient. Resilient. Dusting ourselves off after a freight train like it was a spill. {deadpan} The bull is fine. The hat has a subscription.

## Bit 3 — hope
Inflation is not making things expensive. Things were always expensive. It is making hope expensive. Hope is a luxury item now: one perfect truffle, under glass, for people with serious money. The rest of us manifest. We manifest abundance every morning, and by the evening we have manifested a deeper level of debt. Rent went up this week. The apartment did not. {aside} The floor is sticky in a way that means something happened here. We traded the struggle, the actual messy struggle, for a routine. A comfortable one. Slightly scented. And there is a fence round the whole pasture. Barbed wire. You never see it; they feed you enough not to look. Avocado toast. Outrage. Infographics. {deadpan} The fence is called Inflation. It went up too.

## Bit 4 — the bell
And then the wellness. Crystals. Oils. Thousands on crystals and oils so the dread feels more harmonious. We are tuning out the noise by adding a different frequency of noise. It is a bell. A tiny, expensive, decorative bell. On a skyscraper. A collapsing one. {lean} It does not stop the fall. It makes the descent more aesthetically pleasing. {pace} And we know. That is the thing. We walk into the showroom, the well-lit, slightly overpriced showroom where the wilderness used to be, and we nod. "Oh yes. This is where we are. This is where we are connecting our narratives." Connecting. Our narratives. In a showroom. Nodding. Paying. {shriek} We are the bell. {aside} The light in here is unforgiving; it finds every patch of dust. (she waits) {deadpan} Ding.

## Bit 5 — the tyre
We know about everything. Geopolitics. Blockchain. The difference between a parasympathetic and a sympathetic response. Ask anyone in this room to change a flat tyre. (beat) We swapped competence for content. We cannot sharpen a knife, but we can articulate a nuanced emotional state about the knife. The algorithm picks what we think, what we buy, what colour the wall is. There used to be a bench. A wooden one. It worked. Now there is a sustainable, low-impact outdoor seating solution, and it needs a twenty-minute guided meditation and three artisanal certifications before you are allowed to sit. {aside} Somebody is wearing overpriced moisturiser over a full day of sweat, and the sweat is winning. We are running on vibes. Vibes and vibes and vibes, and structurally unsound: a skyscraper on a foundation of excellent intentions and lukewarm kombucha. {deadpan} The bench is still there. Nobody is certified.

## Bit 6 — the egg
We can talk about existential angst in three languages. Three. Ask us to boil an egg and we open a tutorial. That is the whole species now: emotionally literate, fluent in jargon, mute at the stove. I carried letters through a city that banned paper. I know exactly where I stand with an egg. {lean} You want to know what happened to the egg. I followed the tutorial. Twelve minutes. A voiceover about presence. {aside} The mic smells of everyone who was honest into it before me. {deadpan} It came out beige.

> It came out beige.
