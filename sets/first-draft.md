# Mira stomps to the mic (the first draft)

The first draft, as it arrived (from "Mira stomps to the mic.docx.pdf", 2026-09-10). Kept whole, repeats and all, so the punch-up in `first-draft-punched.md` can be judged against it.

---

(Stomps to the mic. Doesn't wait. Voice is a jagged, high-wire tension, dripping with profanity and absolute contempt. The rhythm is fast, punchy, relentless. No pauses for breath, just a continuous stream of hostile, vivid, absurd imagery.)

Listen to me, you over-leveraged, beige-clad, soul-sucking pieces of shit!

We are living in a goddamn *joke*. A sick, twisted, overpriced, deeply pathetic joke that someone wrote while high on expensive espresso and corporate synergy.

Look at us! Look at this fucking mess! We are all just walking around in these perfectly curated, aesthetically pleasing little shells, these Instagram profiles that are so aggressively *beige* they make me want to punch them in the face! We're all pretending we have it together! We're all pretending that our little life—our carefully selected, ethically sourced, "mindful" little life—is somehow *meaningful*.

**Bullshit!** It's a goddamn hamster wheel made of veneer!

I look at the world, and it's like someone took a massive, beautiful, terrifying beast—like a bull, or maybe a wild mustang—and they strapped a tiny, sparkly party hat on it. And then they started feeding it organic kale and calling it a "wellness journey."

**A party hat on a bull!** That's what we've done to our existence! We've taken the raw, magnificent, terrifying *animal* nature of being alive—the need to bleed, the need to fight, the need to just *BE*—and we've stuffed it into a little, cute, manageable little package.

It's like we've replaced the thunder of the storm with the gentle *tinkle* of wind chimes.

We're supposed to be *resilient*, right? We're supposed to bounce back! But what we're actually doing is just nervously dusting ourselves off after every little setback, like we've had a minor spill instead of being pummeled by a runaway freight train.

And the *money*! Oh, the goddamn money! Inflation isn't just making things expensive; it's making *hope* expensive! Hope is now a luxury item, like a single, perfect truffle. You have to have serious cash just to feel slightly optimistic.

We're all trying to "manifest" abundance, but what we're really manifesting is a deeper level of debt! We are sacrificing our actual, messy, beautiful *struggle* for a very comfortable, slightly scented *routine*.

We are all just walking around, slightly anxious, slightly over-caffeinated, pretending that a five-star review on your life is the same as actual, tangible, glorious, chaotic *existence*.

And the *wellness* angle! Jesus Christ. We've taken the ancient, brutal need to simply *survive* and repackaged it as "mindfulness." We're spending thousands of dollars on crystals and oils to try and make our existential dread feel a little more *harmonious*. We're trying to tune out the noise by adding a different frequency of noise! It's like putting a tiny, expensive decorative bell on a collapsing skyscraper. It doesn't stop the fall, but it certainly makes the descent *more aesthetically pleasing*.

It's a performance! We are all actors in this badly written, underfunded play, and the script is just "Be vaguely concerned, but also look good doing it."

I want the raw, unfiltered, sweat-drenched, slightly offensive truth. I want the kind of news that doesn't sound like a mood board. I want the kind that makes you want to throw your phone across the room.

We've traded the magnificent, terrifying *wilderness* of real life for a very well-lit, slightly overpriced *showroom*.

And we keep walking into it, nodding along, saying, "Oh, yes. This is where we are. This is where we're 'connecting' our narratives."

It's a damn sad, hilarious, and utterly ridiculous spectacle.

We are all just domesticated barnyard animals, kept in vast, climate-controlled arenas. We're fed enough to keep us docile—enough avocado toast, enough outrage bait, enough beautifully designed infographics—that we never actually have to confront the barbed wire fence around the pasture.

The fence, by the way, is called "Inflation," and it's taller and thicker than ever.

We've become this strange hybrid creature: we have the delicate, fragile appearance of a porcelain doll, but underneath, we're all wired with the restless, desperate, primal energy of a cornered badger. And we keep trying to calm that badger down with a lavender essential oil diffuser.

It's a farce. It's a gilded cage where the bars are made of good intentions and the view is always slightly blurred by the sheen of corporate gloss.

We've replaced the glorious, terrifying *wilderness* of real life with a very well-lit, slightly overpriced *showroom*.

And we keep walking into it, nodding along, saying, "Oh, yes. This is where we are. This is where we're 'connecting' our narratives."

It's a damn sad, hilarious, and utterly ridiculous spectacle.

(aside, unprompted: "The dust in the back room smells like old tobacco and forgotten ambition. It settles everywhere, thick as cheap gravy.")

(The mic seems to shudder under the force of her energy. She is breathing hard, her voice a raw, high-tension shriek of exasperation. This is the absolute, unhinged, maximum-volume delivery.)

Are you for real? You want the dirt? You want the unfiltered, goddamn, visceral truth? Fine. Pull up a fucking stool and strap in, because this is going to be a tidal wave of absolute, magnificent bullshit.

We are in a fucking catastrophe, people! A spectacular, expensive, deeply embarrassing catastrophe!

Look at this goddamn culture! We are living in this bizarre, delicate, heavily filtered little bubble, and it is suffocating us to death. We are all pretending we are living some kind of spiritual, artisanal fucking dream, when in reality, we are all just desperately paddling around in tiny, overpriced rubber boats called "Self-Actualization."

The system? The whole fucking machine? It is a colossal, sputtering, narcissistic pile of garbage. It's a glittering, self-congratulatory pile of shit that thinks it's a masterpiece.

We used to *be* big. We used to be loud. We used to be unapologetically fucking messy. We were raw, like a wound that was finally allowed to breathe. We were like a magnificent, feral fucking beast, and we were glorious in our desperation.

Now? Now we've tamed ourselves into highly polished, perpetually stressed-out decorative furniture! We have replaced the deep, bellowing roar of the wilderness with the little, delicate *ding* of a notification.

We are domesticated, I tell you! We are pet projects. We are creatures that have been conditioned to seek out tiny, manageable bursts of serotonin—the little online *likes*, the little mindful moments—while the tectonic plates of our actual lives are shifting and grinding us into dust.

We're not just having a rough patch; we're stuck in a perpetual, gilded shit-show!

And the *pretension*! Holy fuck, the sheer, staggering *pretension*! We have taken the desperate, animal need to just *exist*—the terrifying, beautiful, gut-punching *drive*—and we've wrapped it in ethically sourced cotton and called it "mindfulness."

We are all desperately seeking "resonance," which is just a fancy word for "I hope my problems align nicely with the current cultural trend." We're trying to tune out the howling, undeniable noise of systemic failure by listening to a gentle, synthesized flute loop! It's like putting a tiny, fluffy pillow on a crack in the dam—it's cute, but it does absolutely nothing against the impending fucking flood!

The whole concept of struggle has been digitized, man. It's been branded. It's a *brand*. We want the narrative of the "hard journey," but we demand the perfect, filterable, aesthetically pleasing *photo* of the journey.

We are trading the glorious, exhausting, unpredictable *life* for a beautifully organized, slightly sterile *schedule*.

It's a cosmic joke, and the punchline is that we are all paying full price for the ticket!

We are all walking around, pretending that our carefully chosen life story is a masterpiece, when in reality, we are just a collection of well-intentioned, deeply anxious, wildly over-caffeinated fucking compromises.

And the sheer, goddamn *waste* of it all! The potential, the noise, the sheer magnificent *fucking chaos* that we are actively suppressing in favor of "balance."

It's not just a mess; it's a beautifully decorated, impeccably marketed, soul-crushing fucking mess!

(aside, unprompted: "The smell of sweat and overpriced moisturizer hangs heavy in the air. It's a specific kind of tired.")

(The pace doesn't slow; if anything, it accelerates, becoming a furious, breathless torrent. She sounds like she's arguing with the entire population of the planet at once, her contempt absolute.)

And this leads us right to the biggest, most spectacular, most breathtakingly idiotic piece of this entire fucking puzzle: the total, utter, magnificent *loss* of common sense!

Where the hell is the practical knowledge, I ask you? Where is the muscle memory? Where is the ability to just *know* how to fix a goddamn thing?

We've traded competence for curated content. We've swapped the ability to sharpen a tool or even reliably cook a decent meal for the ability to articulate a nuanced emotional state about that tool or that meal. It's a goddamn catastrophic regression!

We have become a species of highly sophisticated, emotionally literate, utterly useless armchair philosophers.

The whole world has become a glorified, high-budget Wikipedia entry! We know *about* everything—the geopolitical ramifications, the subtle nuances of blockchain technology, the difference between a parasympathetic and sympathetic response—but we have absolutely no idea how to, for Christ's sake, change a flat tire!

We're so busy trying to *optimize* our inner landscape that we've forgotten how to navigate the goddamn landscape outside our windows!

The skillset of the modern adult is apparently defined by the quality of their mood board. We are fluent in jargon, but utterly mute in practicality. We are so reliant on algorithms to tell us what to think, what to buy, and even what color to paint the fucking wall.

It's like we've decided that the ability to read a map is less important than the ability to share a beautifully filtered picture of a sunset. And I swear, the sunset is still just a sunset, but we've elevated it to a profound, deeply personal, self-help *experience*!

We have replaced the robust, messy, tangible wisdom of "knowing how things work" with the flimsy, abstract wisdom of "knowing how to *feel* about things."

We've lost the magnificent, gritty skill of just *doing*.

We've got this obsession with "being present," which, let's be real, is often just a highly refined way of saying, "I am currently holding my phone and pretending to be serene."

We've traded the certainty of a well-made, sturdy wooden bench for the vague promise of a "sustainable, low-impact outdoor seating solution." The bench works! The promise, however, requires a twenty-minute guided meditation session and three different artisanal certifications.

It's a fucking farce!

We are running on vibes and vibes and vibes, but we are structurally unsound! We are trying to build a skyscraper on a foundation made of excellent intentions and lukewarm kombucha.

We are a civilization that knows how to talk about existential angst in three languages, but can't even reliably boil an egg without consulting a YouTube tutorial. That is the grand, tragic, deeply hilarious fucking punchline of the modern human condition!

(aside, unprompted: "The light here is unforgiving. It reveals every patch of unnecessary dust.")

(Mira, backstage, unprompted: "The noise level in this venue suggests a desperate need for a very strong drink.")
