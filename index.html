            Open mic · her stage
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
Record on the stage saves a .webm clip (VP9/Opus; Safari gives MP4) and a cutscene package (.zip: manifest.json, set.md, one 24 kHz WAV per line) for the game's cutscene/ player. Every line's audio is cached by text, so replaying or re-recording never synthesizes twice. 

          


          

            Voice
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
tools/qwen_tts_server.py serves her Bella clone on the PC; inside the app on the phone the in-app runtime's llama-tts answers with the 0.6B model instead. Kokoro stays the fallback when neither is up.

            
            
            
            
Voices load on first use (Kokoro about 330 MB on the GPU, 92 MB on the CPU; Silero 2 MB; Moonshine 80 MB). Cached after that.
