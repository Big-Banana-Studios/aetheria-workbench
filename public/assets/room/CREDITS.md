# The room's sounds

Recorded people, from Wikimedia Commons, cut to short clips and encoded as
mono Ogg Vorbis for the comedy stage (`src/openmic/room.js`). Nothing here is
a recording of anyone in the family.

| clips | source | licence |
|---|---|---|
| `big-1` … `big-4`, `big-short`, `clapter` | "approx 800 laughter" recordings by **lonemonk** (freesound.org), via Commons files 72842, 72843, 72844 | CC BY 3.0 |
| `medium-1` | "Lachkonserve 1" by Sleepytomcat (Jens Kraglund) | public domain |
| `medium-2` | "Small group laughter" by Ch0cchi | CC BY 3.0 |
| `one-1` … `one-3` | "Bursting out laughing" by ezwa | public domain |
| `one-4` … `one-6` | "Laughter(s)" by sagetyrtle | CC0 |
| `applause` | "Slow starting applause" by stephan | public domain |
| `club-bed` | "Cafe ambiance" by Marble Toast (45 s of a 20-minute recording) | CC0 |

The murmur under a laugh and the room's swell are synthesized in the app.
